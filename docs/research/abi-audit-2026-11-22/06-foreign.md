## Swift receiver and dispatch

### F01 — Split context Self, direct Self, and absent Self (partial, source risk)

IRGen's hasSelfContextParameter first checks formal indirectness, then metatype representation, classes/class-bounded substituted types and ObjC existential types. A direct value or existential metatype can travel in the ordinary bank; a thin metatype can contribute zero words. expandParameters emits ordinary parameters including direct Self, then polymorphic arguments, then context. Throwing functions can reserve context even without context Self. arm64 assigns the attributed SwiftSelf pointer x20. [Swift GenCall.cpp:1891–1917](https://github.com/swiftlang/swift/blob/83eb12448e4323abbf81b0153dced2faff02d22f/lib/IRGen/GenCall.cpp#L1891-L1917) [Swift GenCall.cpp:2020–2107](https://github.com/swiftlang/swift/blob/83eb12448e4323abbf81b0153dced2faff02d22f/lib/IRGen/GenCall.cpp#L2020-L2107) [LLVM AArch64CallingConvention.td:242–243](https://github.com/swiftlang/llvm-project/blob/fb5f84d903a3b4e7aea15cd5666598a46f56c833/llvm/lib/Target/AArch64/AArch64CallingConvention.td#L242-L243)

- Consumer. - Oracle and mutation. ### F02 — Mutation, borrowing, consuming and specialization (partial)

SIL conventions distinguish inout, owned, guaranteed and other indirect forms. IRGen classifies formal indirectness without requiring mutation; a direct consuming value may have ordinary-bank Self. Specialization including exploded/dead Self overrides the original entry shape. [Swift SILFunctionType.cpp:1450–1524](https://github.com/swiftlang/swift/blob/83eb12448e4323abbf81b0153dced2faff02d22f/lib/SIL/IR/SILFunctionType.cpp#L1450-L1524) [Swift GenCall.cpp:1891–1917](https://github.com/swiftlang/swift/blob/83eb12448e4323abbf81b0153dced2faff02d22f/lib/IRGen/GenCall.cpp#L1891-L1917)

- Change after F01 and report 01. Separate ownership/inout/read-write effects from carrier. Resolve entry-specific specialization before allocating receiver. Hold source ownership unknown if the compiler's convention cannot be recovered.
- Oracle and mutation. Compile mutating/borrowing/consuming methods on copyable and move-only structs, resilient exported member and specialized generic versions. Include read-only indirect Self beside mutating Self and native counters for deinitialization. Swap direct and indirect receiver placement or mark an indirect read-only argument inout and require a dedicated failure.
