# Async ABI audit

Read-only audit of swiftdc dev b404252 plus the runtime-identity, terminal-endpoint and acceptance working files. Verified the release source checkout is detached at 83eb12448e4323abbf81b0153dced2faff02d22f. This report proposes implementation and verification, with no runtime execution or new build performed here. Its source boundary is native Darwin arm64/arm64e Swift and the supported Swift concurrency runtime/provider family. Versioned or unavailable contracts fall back to raw rendering.

## Implementation order and acceptance

1. **Use the same evidence loader in grouped APIs.** Keep the WIP binding/endpoint separation. Feed identity into the proof pass before ordinary annotations, and preserve structural candidates for diagnostics. Stream already calls on-demand at Disassembler.swift:1133–1158; assemble's index has an on-demand fallback at :1813, but its state analysis uses the filtered map at :1844. This is a confirmed dependency-availability mismatch, distinct from currently unimplemented hop seeding. Supply one region-scoped loader with bounds, local routine/thunk decoding and attached role information; the analysis region can include dependencies without emitting them. Share validated stub/provider and descriptor facts. Cover full array, filtered by entry and by resume where accepted, stream, and stripped whole image. The census at :1530–1550 intentionally has hints only. Specify a separate negative/no-claimed-effects contract for census and unsupported dyld-cache providers unless equivalent decoded-stub/fixup proof is implemented. Preserve bounded residency and measure extra decodes on a large input. Source for this change is swiftdc, not a Swift ABI difference.
2. **Recover typed edge state and scoped lifetime.** Extend raw branch replay at ValueTracking.swift:1866–1912 and seed construction at :1249–1328 without publishing proof on enriched/decorated register facts. Track scoped task and allocation identity (owner, site and generation/call instance), range and full-width offset identities, live state, Parent and Resume words, exact pointer copies and escaped reach. Use byte-accurate invalidation for partial and overlapping writes. Keep definite words by intersection and possible aliases/publications by union. On an unknown or incomplete predecessor, omitted second edge, missing detail, nonconvergence, unknown call or unresolved jump, withhold positive seeds unless independently proved. Replay relevant in-image calls boundedly through their true returns, carrying explicit operands, hidden context, child headers and captured memory. Stop initially at recursive/reentrant or multiple-live site instances. Never substitute a fresh local capture bitmap for inherited reach. Publish through an older child Parent relation means the owner is reachable even after header repair, stack spill clobber, child death or a later x22 reload. Distinguish a pointer copied within a private frame from durable external reach. Invalidate unknown memory according to its accessible objects, keeping user buffers/inout/captures distinct from private payload. Ordinary dealloc proves exact current-task live top base and retires aliases without reviving facts on reload; through-dealloc needs proof of the suffix it retires.
3. **Deliver the first useInt consumer.** Trace the actual optimized fixture, with dwarf/symbol/assembly addresses treated as fixture-specific. At 0xe6c–0xe94 owner O allocates C, stores C at O+0x10, writes C.Parent=O and C.Resume=0xe98 and hands off to intResult. Its entry 0xad8 allocates D, writes D.Parent=C and D.Resume=0xb24, and hands D to qualified Yield. At 0xb24 the callee restores C, exactly deallocates D and returns through C.Resume with C still live. At 0xe98, prove reloaded C from O+0x10 matches the same live allocation, prove x22 restoration from C.Parent, write the awaited value at O+0x18, and retire C using the qualified dealloc. At 0xee4 prove qualified switch, x22 exactly O, x0 exactly 0xee8, full-width x1/x2 both zero, complete incoming set and absence of reachable writers for the selected word. Seed the scalar at O+0x18 here only, giving the real sink at 0xf04 the expression `AwaitResults.sink((awaited1 + 2))`. The `-Onone` fixture has O+0x20 child and O+0x28 value with O+0x10 holding an owner reload, so recognize dataflow and operands instead of offsets or fixture names. O may be borrowed from an async caller. Prove entry provenance from a complete local allocation/entry edge, or apply an explicitly scoped well-formed compiler-generated Swift ABI entry contract where the private context is handed to it fresh and initialized with Parent/Resume. That contract covers O's own protected payload and its scoped storage; it provides no blanket absence of caller or ancestor capture. Evidence of an unusual native caller, raw ABI exposure, or an unproved external entry contract disables the seed. Begin with a native leaf fixture if this admission is not independently established. Covered callers and runtime maintain private O by supported ABI, not by absence of local markers. Keep existing entry-based proofs audited with the same checks: AsyncContextEscapes.swift:46–53 excludes terminal transfers from unknown-branch handling, while Disassembler.swift:2676–2718 seeds all later funclets from observed chain stores. A raw/native callee traversing C.Parent could invalidate an entry-proven slot. That is a concrete missing interprocedural check; classify an existing rendered wrong value only after the negative is reproduced.
4. **Generalize ordinary awaits and exact ABI locations.** Next prove the second ordinary await in sequence independently via those same entry-to-exact-return summaries; solve ordered successor state over the transfer graph with bounded fixed point and must intersection. Distinguish results from each await origin even at common continuations. Decode native parameter/result schemas from concrete entry/call/resume and descriptor/thunk evidence. Add direct multi-GP/mixed bank, by-address results with proved complete writes/initialization and no aliases, generic/by-address results, typed-throws discriminator and distinct success/error state, and formal/isolation words only when a signature or independent native-use proof supplies their bank/lane. Prove closure/thunk abstraction and capture address, class/witness/override target and allocation-size pair through the actual descriptor/metadata slot and fixup, discriminators and auth operations. Where dynamic target set is open, common convention can support ABI role if canonical, but cannot prove memory preservation without an applicable contract common to all targets. Once actor/custom executor operands have an independently supported route, use the corresponding callback/capture summary. A nonzero executor word in the initial generic-only tier declines.
5. **Add operation-specific runtime and coroutine protocols.** Expand proven endpoint classifier from four initial routines to exact versioned wait, continuation, async-let, task-group, cancellation and coroutine routines only as an effect consumer needs them. Model task/result-buffer identity, initialization and destruction, resume and error alternatives, task-owned allocator vs ordinary coroutine frame and explicit yields, begin/end/abort, duplicate finish/cancel and cancellation handler callback reach. Model continuation init as potential handoff/ownership, prove the later await and proper resume transition, keep completion-before-await (inline) and await-before-completion (scheduled) distinct until their facts agree. Group wait-next can produce nil, child success or child error; wait-all drains children and can propagate saved errors. Async-let finish can still run code and destroy an initialized result. Child or group work may run concurrently; join only initialized result, retain the may-writer facts associated with captures up to proven completion, and retire storage only at its matching lifecycle event. Reuse ABI-shape recovery for positive indexing, but do not infer harmlessness from names or assume all await purposes share result locations.

## Provider and scheduler boundary

A strong import/stub/fixup tuple selects the supported ABI family under ordinary loader rules, comparable to the existing ARC provider assumption. Interpret the pinned runtime by path and effect. Generic switch may park the task and preferred TaskExecutor may still invoke conforming custom scheduling code. Treat task and scheduler possession of an opaque token as resumption authority, distinct from external writable reach to O/C/D. App callbacks may mutate data they already reach. Trace explicit native frame-pointer publication, unsafe projection, user buffers, raw jobs or hooks, demonstrated representation access, and unknown provider as real unknown effects. A raw observer that reinterprets a Job or a Task can leave the supported contract. Mere presence of a task preference, executor callback, task/continuation handle, or the possibility of injected runtime code supplies no such evidence. Require compatible family/availability for backdeployment, platform and task-to-thread routes or decline unproved effects.
